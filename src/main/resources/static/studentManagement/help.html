Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new student?

Go to Students → Add New Student, or click the “Add Student” button in the top bar. Fill in the required fields and save.

Can I export student data?

Yes. On the Students page you can filter the list and use the Export option (coming soon in the full version).

How is attendance calculated?

Attendance percentage is calculated as (Present days ÷ Total scheduled days) × 100 for each student per course.

Who can access the system?

Currently this demo is designed for administrators. Role-based access (teachers, students) can be added later.
